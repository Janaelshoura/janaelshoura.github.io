Customer Churn Prediction Using Machine Learning

Course Developer: Dr. Shaimaa Masry

Teaching Assistants: Ahmed el naggar

Customer churn is a critical business problem where companies lose customers who stop using their

services. Retaining existing customers is often cheaper than acquiring new ones. This project builds a

complete machine learning pipeline to predict whether a customer will churn based on their demographic

information, service usage, and billing details.

Business Objective: - Predict customer churn (Yes/No) - Identify key factors influencing churn - Help

businesses take proactive retention actions

Dataset: Telco Customer Churn (Kaggle)
